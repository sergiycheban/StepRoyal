What to know about the Chisinau - Odesa trip

Chisinau - Odesa is designed for travellers who want a direct door-to-door journey without mandatory changes. Chisinau is Moldova’s capital and the main hub for trips to RMO Airport, Ukraine and Romania; Odesa is a major city in southern Ukraine and a practical starting point for trips across the south, Moldova and Romania.

This is an international journey between Moldova and Ukraine. Border-control time is variable, so a generous connection buffer is recommended when the trip is linked to a meeting or onward transport.

The reference road distance is about 197 km. For practical planning, allow roughly 4-6 h; this is a planning range rather than a guaranteed arrival time and includes room for stops and possible delays.

If your journey connects with a flight, share the flight number and airport when booking.
